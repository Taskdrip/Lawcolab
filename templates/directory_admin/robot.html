How Both Robots Work
Law Firm Robot
  1. Scans Google Maps & public directories for Nigerian law firms
  2. Extracts name, phone, website, practice areas, Google rating
  3. Flags unverified GMB listings and firms without websites
  4. Imports into CRM — skips duplicates automatically
  5. Each firm CRM page generates a custom AI pitch email + call script
Social Communities Robot
  1. Discovers major legal communities on Facebook, LinkedIn, Reddit, WhatsApp, Telegram & YouTube
  2. Captures member count, join links, and how-to-join instructions
  3. Stores in a dedicated Communities CRM with outreach tracking
  4. Generates platform-specific AI messages for each community
  5. Tracks which communities have been contacted and replied
