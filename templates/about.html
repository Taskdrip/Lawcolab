Our Story

Born from the Courtroom,
Built for the Future

2015

Abraham is called to the Bar, beginning a legal career that would expose him to the real inefficiencies hampering law firms across Africa and beyond.

2018–2022

After consulting for dozens of law firms, Abraham partners with Wiam Tahbat and begins researching, prototyping, and building what would become LAWCOLAB — a platform designed by lawyers, for lawyers.

2024

LAWCOLAB Global officially launches under Taskdrip, bringing enterprise-grade legal practice management to law firms across Nigeria, Ghana, the US, UK, and beyond.

Today

We power 150+ law firms across 6 countries — with a global directory of 3,000+ firms and growing. Our mission: make every legal professional a world-class operator.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To empower every legal professional worldwide — from solo practitioners to global law firms — with intelligent technology that streamlines operations, enhances client relationships, and transforms legal practice management from complex to seamless.

Our Vision

A world where every law firm — regardless of size or location — has access to enterprise-grade technology that lets them focus on what they do best: delivering exceptional legal outcomes for their clients and communities.
