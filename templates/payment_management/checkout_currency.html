How It Works

Changing the currency updates the symbol and label shown on the public checkout page.

Bank transfer and crypto instructions will reflect the selected currency.

Price amounts (e.g. 39, 90, 350) remain unchanged — adjust them in Sales → Popup Settings.

All new checkout sessions use the updated currency immediately after saving.
