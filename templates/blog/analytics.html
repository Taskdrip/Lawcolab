Engagement Score Formula
Engagement Score = Views × 1 + Shares × 3 + Comments × 5. Shares and comments receive higher weight because they represent active intent — a reader who shares or comments is far more engaged than one who simply views. Use this score to identify your best-performing content and replicate its structure, tone, and topic depth.
