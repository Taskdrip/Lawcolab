LAWCOLAB v2 – AI Law Firm Growth Engine

Objective

Upgrade the current LAWCOLAB lead generation module into a complete AI-powered business development system that automatically discovers law firms, enriches their profiles, manages outreach, tracks conversations, and converts prospects into LAWCOLAB customers.

The experience must be world-class, mobile-first, and feel more polished than HubSpot, Apollo.io, Instantly, and Clay while remaining simple enough for non-technical staff.

---

1. AI Lead Discovery Engine

Replace the current Google Business scraper with an intelligent Lead Discovery Robot.

The admin should be able to enter natural language searches such as:

- Law firms in Ibadan Nigeria
- Corporate lawyers in Lagos
- Immigration lawyers in London
- Family law firms in Toronto
- Intellectual property attorneys in Dubai
- Barristers in Abuja

The robot should automatically search multiple public sources including:

- Google Business listings
- Google Search
- Bing
- Business directories
- Legal directories
- Chamber of Commerce websites
- Bar association listings
- Public law firm websites
- Yellow Pages
- Yelp (where available)
- LinkedIn public company pages
- Other publicly available legal directories

As results are discovered, automatically deduplicate firms before saving them.
